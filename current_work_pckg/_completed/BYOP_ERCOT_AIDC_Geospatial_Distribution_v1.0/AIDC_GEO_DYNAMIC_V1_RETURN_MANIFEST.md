## Points for the researcher to review

1. **Locking**: only 387 MW IT of observed, operating capacity is locked. Abilene buildings 5–8, Shackelford, the Crusoe expansion and Helios Phase II/III remain forecast, and their dynamic allocation falls clearly below the legacy one. Any of them can be moved into `locked_verified` in the YAML.

3. **POI proxies for new buses**: earliest COD 2028 against first demand in 2027, and a loose 2,000 MW upgrade cap per bus.
4. **p650 7 has no wind profile**: the bus currently gets no onsite wind. Choose whether to borrow a neighbouring bus's profile or leave it as is.
